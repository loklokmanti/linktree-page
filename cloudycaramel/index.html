FROM GUJI.TO YOUR CUP.

Over the last couple of months, I’ve been tasting different coffees and roasts to find one I’d love to share with you guys.

After a lot of tasting, I landed on a coffee from Guji, Ethiopia, lightly roasted to let its natural flavours shine through. It’s become a favourite of mine, and I can’t wait for you to try this super aromatic coffee.

01 / IN THE CUP

TASTE

Expect caramel and milk chocolate sweetness, a bit of tropical fruit, and a little citrus to keep it fresh. Fruity, sweet and nicely balanced. From filter to espresso, these versatile beans are made for your favourite way to brew.

02 / WHERE IT BEGINS

ORIGIN

Guji is a coffee-growing region in southern Ethiopia, home to the Guji Oromo people. Many families there have been growing coffee for generations, on small farms alongside other crops and under the shade of trees. Coffee has been part of life here for a long time.

The green beans are sourced through Trabocca from a farm certified Organic, Fairtrade and Rainforest Alliance.
